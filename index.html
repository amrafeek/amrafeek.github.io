FAQ

What is a transparent PNG?

A PNG image with an alpha channel, so the area around the subject is see-through. You can place it on any background without a white box.

Are the images free for commercial use?

Yes. ClikPNG images are free for personal and commercial projects. Trademarked brand logos are for editorial use only, such as reviews and news.

Do I need an account to download?

No. Open any image on ClikPNG and click download.

Can I make my own transparent PNG?

Yes. Upload your photo to the free background remover and download the result as PNG.
